About What’s A Good Deal?

A deals site for PC gamers and tech buyers that tries to answer one question honestly: is this price actually good?

How deals get here

  • Checked every hour. Prices come straight from the stores (mostly Newegg, plus sales on brands’ own sites such as Logitech, Razer and Keychron). A deal that can’t be re-verified for three days is removed.
  • Real discounts only. Items are listed when the store shows a genuine price cut, and refurbished, open-box and poorly rated items are filtered out.
  • Right category. Every product is re-checked each hour so a prebuilt PC never shows up under SSDs.

Honest about discounts

Stores often compare against a “was” price nobody actually paid. We keep every product’s price history, so each deal links to a chart of what it really cost. Once a product has enough history, cards show when today’s price is the lowest in 30 days, how far it is below its usual price, and a warning when the store’s “was” price is higher than anything it charged recently.

Builds and value rankings

The Builds tab assembles complete AMD and Intel gaming PCs from today’s prices every hour, matching sockets, memory type, case size and power. Best Value ranks graphics cards and processors by approximate gaming performance per dollar. Performance scores are our own rough estimates, not benchmarks of your games.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t invent prices, ratings or dates. If we can’t verify something, we leave it out.
  • Stores don’t pay to be listed or ranked.
  • We don’t currently earn anything from links. If that changes (for example through store affiliate programs), it will be stated here and on the site, and it won’t change which deals are shown.

Prices change fast. Always confirm the final price in the store’s cart before you buy.
