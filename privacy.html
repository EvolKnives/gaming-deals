Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no tracking, nothing collected about you.

What we collect

Nothing. This site has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies.

What stays on your device

A few preferences are kept in your browser’s own storage and never sent to us: deals you Save (and their price when you saved them), the PC you put together in Build your own, and whether you closed the Black Friday banner. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

Links to stores

Deal buttons take you to the store’s own website, which has its own privacy policy. When you share a custom build, the link contains only the parts you picked.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security. Product photos load directly from the stores’ and brands’ image servers (for example Newegg’s), which see the same basic request information any website does. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

Changes

If anything here changes, for example if the site starts using store affiliate links, this page will be updated first.
